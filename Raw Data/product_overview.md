# Product Overview

## Northstar Assist

Northstar Assist is a cloud-based AI support platform designed to help customer service teams answer questions and manage internal knowledge.

## Main Features

The platform includes an AI chat assistant, searchable knowledge base, analytics dashboard, and workflow automation tools.

The AI assistant can retrieve information from approved company documentation before generating an answer.

## Knowledge Sources

Administrators can upload supported documents to the knowledge base.

Documents are processed, divided into smaller sections, and indexed so relevant information can be retrieved when a user asks a question.

## User Access

Standard users can search approved knowledge and interact with the AI assistant.

Administrators can manage knowledge sources, review usage analytics, and configure organization settings.

## Security

Northstar Assist supports role-based access control and multi-factor authentication.

Customer data is logically separated between organizations.

## Availability

The service is designed for continuous availability, although planned maintenance may occasionally occur outside peak business hours.

## Support

Customers can contact support through the in-app support portal.
