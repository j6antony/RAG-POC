# Business Expense Policy

## Purpose

The company reimburses employees for reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred while performing approved work activities.

## Eligible Expenses

Common eligible expenses include approved business travel, accommodation, client meals, conference registration, and required office supplies.

Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for individual expenses of $25 or more.

Employees should submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase or completion of business travel.

## Meals

When travelling for business, employees may claim reasonable meal expenses.

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client event.

## Travel

Flights and hotels should be booked through the approved corporate travel provider when possible.

Employees should generally choose economy airfare for flights under six hours.

## Manager Approval

Expenses above $500 require manager approval before purchase unless they are part of a previously approved travel plan.

## Reimbursement

Approved expenses are normally reimbursed through payroll within two pay periods after the expense report is approved.
