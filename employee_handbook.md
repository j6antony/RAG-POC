# Employee Handbook

## Overview

Welcome to Northstar Labs. This handbook provides general information about workplace expectations, employee responsibilities, communication, attendance, and professional conduct.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

Flexible start and end times may be arranged with a manager when business needs allow.

## Attendance

Employees should notify their manager as early as possible if they will be late or absent. Repeated unreported absences may be reviewed by management.

## Professional Conduct

Employees are expected to communicate respectfully with coworkers, customers, and business partners. Harassment, discrimination, and threatening behaviour are not permitted.

## Confidential Information

Employees must protect confidential company, customer, and partner information. Confidential data should only be accessed when required for legitimate work purposes.

## Company Equipment

Company laptops, phones, and other devices are provided primarily for business use. Employees are responsible for taking reasonable steps to prevent loss or damage.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT and the employee's manager as soon as possible.

## Time Off

Vacation, sick leave, and personal leave are covered by separate company policies. Employees should review the appropriate policy before requesting time away from work.

## Questions

Employees should contact their manager or Human Resources if they are unsure about a policy or workplace expectation.
