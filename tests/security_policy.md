# Information Security Policy

## Account Security

All employees must use multi-factor authentication when accessing company systems that support it.

Passwords must contain at least 12 characters and should not be reused across personal and company accounts.

Employees must never share passwords, authentication codes, or active login sessions with another person.

## Remote Access

Employees accessing internal systems outside a company office must use the approved corporate VPN when required by IT.

Remote access may also require multi-factor authentication.

## Company Devices

Company devices must use screen locking and disk encryption.

Employees should install security updates when prompted and must not disable endpoint protection software.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Sensitive Data

Sensitive customer and company data must only be stored in approved systems.

Employees should not copy confidential data to personal cloud storage, personal email accounts, or unauthorized USB drives.

## Phishing

Suspicious emails, unexpected login prompts, and suspected phishing attempts should be reported to the security team.

Employees should not open unexpected attachments or enter credentials into unfamiliar websites.
