# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role and team responsibilities allow it.

Managers may require additional in-office attendance for training, customer meetings, planning sessions, or other business needs.

## Work Location

Employees should normally work remotely from their registered home location.

Remote work from another province or country requires advance approval from both the employee's manager and Human Resources.

## Availability

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

Employees should maintain reliable internet access and an appropriate work environment.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must follow all company information security requirements.

When accessing internal systems remotely, employees must use the corporate VPN when required and complete multi-factor authentication.

Confidential discussions should not be conducted in public areas where they can easily be overheard.

## Equipment

Approved employees may use company-issued laptops and accessories while working remotely.

Technical problems with company equipment should be reported to IT support.
