# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## First Day

New employees should meet with their manager, complete required employment documentation, and confirm access to company systems.

IT will provide company equipment and account credentials.

## Account Setup

Employees must set a new password during initial account setup.

Multi-factor authentication must be configured before accessing protected company applications.

## Required Training

New employees must complete security awareness, privacy, and workplace conduct training during their first two weeks.

Some teams may require additional role-specific training.

## Equipment

New employees are normally issued a company laptop.

Employees working remotely may also receive approved accessories such as a monitor, keyboard, or headset depending on role requirements.

## Remote Employees

Employees approved for remote work should test their VPN connection before they need access to internal systems outside the office.

Remote work remains subject to the company's Remote Work Policy.

## Benefits and Time Off

Human Resources will provide information about benefits, vacation, sick leave, and other employee programs.

Full vacation rules are described in the Vacation Policy.

## Support

Questions about accounts, devices, or software should be sent to IT support.

Questions about benefits, leave, and employment policies should be directed to Human Resources.
