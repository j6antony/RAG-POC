# Vacation Policy

## Annual Vacation Allowance

Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days per calendar year.

Employees who begin work partway through the year receive a prorated vacation allowance based on their start date.

## Requesting Vacation

Vacation requests should normally be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the company's time-off system.

Requests are reviewed based on team coverage, project deadlines, and business requirements.

Vacation longer than 10 consecutive business days requires manager approval.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year.

Any vacation days above the 5-day carryover limit expire at the end of the calendar year unless an exception is approved by Human Resources.

## Blackout Periods

Some teams may have limited vacation availability during major product launches, audits, or other critical business periods.

Managers should communicate blackout periods as early as possible.

## Sick Leave

Vacation days should not normally be used for illness. Sick leave is managed separately from vacation time.
