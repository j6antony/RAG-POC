Part 4 – Draft an Introduction
Knowledge and project information can be created over the course of a project.
AIDAS Technologies is a data and analytics company that helps organizations solve business-related problems using data, AI, and ML (AIDAS Technologies, n.d.). I am currently working as an intern at AIDAS in product development. My responsibilities include researching new technologies, developing proofs of concept, documenting my technical work, and working with team members. My work requires continuous research and experimentation, which means that a large amount of technical knowledge and context is required to understand the project.
A huge challenge that arises from this role is keeping this technical information organized in an easily accessible format for everyone working on the project. My information may be stored across many different documents, code files, notes, and conversations. As a result, team members may need to spend additional time conducting research on their own and understanding work that has already been done. This creates duplicated effort and makes it difficult for someone new to the project to quickly understand the project.
From my research, I have learned that this is a common issue in software and technical workplaces. Bjørnson and Dingsøyr (2008) describe software engineering as knowledge-intensive work and found that managing and sharing information is an important concern within software organizations. Their research also found that simply storing information is not sufficient, as both documented knowledge and knowledge held by individual team members should be considered. From this, it is clear that changing the way that project information is documented, organized, and shared can greatly reduce the barrier to collaboration that is present.
The objective of this report will be to evaluate the methods available for organizing and sharing technical information within a product development team. Additionally, this report will recommend an approach that is optimal for communicating information to team members. The report will take into consideration factors such as accessibility, ease of use, consistency, and the amount of effort required to create and maintain the documentation. Improving these processes will reduce the time spent searching for information, avoid duplicate work, and improve team collaboration. Overall, this will increase the team’s efficiency.
